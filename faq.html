FAQ

Orders

How do I place an order?

Add products to your bag, tap Checkout, and enter your details. You will get a confirmation email as soon as your order goes through.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, as long as it has not been dispatched. Email us as soon as possible with your order number. Orders placed before 2pm on a weekday usually leave the same day.

I have not received a confirmation email.

Check your spam or promotions folder first. If it is not there, email us and we will confirm your order and resend the details.

Do I need an account?

No. You can check out as a guest using just your email address.

Delivery

How much is delivery?

Royal Mail Tracked 48 is £3.95, or free on orders over £40. Tracked 24 is £5.95. See Delivery and returns for full details.

How long will my order take?

Orders placed before 2pm on a weekday are dispatched the same day. Tracked 48 usually arrives in 2 to 3 working days and Tracked 24 in 1 to 2 working days.

Do you deliver outside the UK?

Not yet. We currently deliver to UK addresses only.

Is the packaging discreet?

Yes. Orders are sent in plain, recyclable outer packaging.

Products

Are your products tested?

Yes. Every batch is tested by an independent laboratory for identity, strength and purity before it is released for sale.

Where are your products made?

Our products are made in GMP-certified facilities in the UK.

Are your products vegan?

Many are. Each product page shows whether it is vegan in the details. Products such as whey protein, marine collagen and fish oil are not vegan.

Can I take more than one product together?

Most of our products are designed to be taken alongside each other, but always read the label and do not exceed the stated daily dose. If you take medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a medical condition, speak to your doctor or pharmacist before taking food supplements.

Are your products suitable for children?

No. Unless the label says otherwise, our supplements are intended for adults aged 18 and over.

How should I store my products?

Keep them in a cool, dry place out of direct sunlight, with the lid closed, and out of the reach of children.

Returns and refunds

What is your returns policy?

You can return unopened, sealed items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. See Returns.

My item arrived damaged.

Sorry about that. Email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we will send a replacement or refund.

How long do refunds take?

We process refunds within 14 days of receiving your return. Your bank may take a few extra working days to show it.

Payment

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept major debit and credit cards. Available options are shown at checkout.

Is checkout secure?

Yes. Payments are handled by our payment provider over an encrypted connection. We never see or store your full card details.

Do you have discount codes?

Sign up to the Forma list at the bottom of our homepage and we will send you 10% off your first order.

Still need help? Get in touch and a real person will reply within one working day.
