This policy explains how [Company name] Ltd ("Forma", "we", "us") collects and uses your personal data when you visit our website or buy from us. We are the data controller for this information and we follow the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, email address, delivery address, phone number (if given) and what you bought.

Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted providers who help us run Forma, and only as much as they need:

  • Our payment provider, to take payment securely.
  • Royal Mail and our fulfilment partner, to deliver your order.
  • Our website hosting, email and analytics providers.
  • Professional advisers and authorities where the law requires it.

Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as UK adequacy regulations or approved contract clauses.

We keep order and transaction records for 6 years to meet tax and accounting rules. Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe. Contact form messages are kept for up to 2 years.

  • Marketing cookies: to measure our advertising. We only set these with your consent.

You can change your choices at any time in your browser settings.
