Delivery and returns

Free UK delivery on orders over £40. Returns on unopened items within 30 days.

Last updated 19 October 2026
DeliveryReturnsRefundsFaulty itemsCancelling

Delivery

We deliver to addresses in the United Kingdom. Orders placed before 2pm, Monday to Friday are packed and dispatched the same working day. Orders placed after 2pm, at weekends or on bank holidays are dispatched the next working day.

ServiceDelivery timeCost
Royal Mail Tracked 482 to 3 working days£3.95, or free over £40
Royal Mail Tracked 241 to 2 working days£5.95

You will receive a confirmation email with your order details, followed by a dispatch email with a tracking link once your parcel is on its way.

Delays

Delivery times are estimates from Royal Mail and are not guaranteed. During busy periods, such as December, deliveries can take a little longer. If your order has not arrived within 7 working days of dispatch, please contact us and we will look into it with the courier.

Missing or damaged parcels

If your parcel arrives damaged, or an item is missing, let us know within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo where possible. We will send a replacement or issue a refund.

Returns

If you are not happy with your order, you can return any unopened item in its original, sealed condition within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price.

For health and hygiene reasons, we cannot accept returns of food supplements, food or skincare once the seal has been broken, unless the item is faulty.

How to return an item

  • Email hello@[yourdomain] with your order number and the items you would like to return.
  • We will reply with the returns address and a returns reference.
  • Pack the items securely and send them back using a tracked service. Please keep your proof of postage.

Return postage is paid by you unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong product.

Refunds

Once your return arrives and has been checked, we will email you to confirm and refund the item price to your original payment method within 14 days. Your bank may take a few extra working days to show the refund.

If you return your whole order within 14 days of delivery, we will also refund the standard delivery charge you paid.

Faulty or incorrect items

If an item is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery and we will offer a replacement or a full refund, including any postage costs. This does not affect your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel your order at any time before it is dispatched by emailing us. Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 you also have the right to cancel within 14 days of receiving your goods. This right does not apply to sealed health and hygiene products once they have been unsealed after delivery. Full details are in our Terms of sale.
